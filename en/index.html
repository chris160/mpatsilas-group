Our black pig

The treasure
of the farm.

The Greek black pig is an old, hardy breed of the Greek mountains. On our farm it grows slowly, roams freely and feeds on what the mountain provides.

The result is deep red meat with intense flavour and fat that melts in the mouth, rich in unsaturated fats, including Omega-3. Nutritious, delicious and special: meat you'll rarely find anywhere else.

  • Free-range in the mountains of Aetia
  • Slow, natural growth
  • Rich in unsaturated fats & Omega-3
  • Available at our butcher shop and tavern

Vasilitsa · Pindus

Next to Vasilitsa.

Aetia lies close to Vasilitsa, one of the most beautiful ski resorts in Greece, with dense forests of black pine and Bosnian pine and views across the Pindus.

In winter, skiing and snowboarding on the Vasilitsa slopes, then warmth by the fire and dinner at the tavern. In summer, hiking, off-road trips, mountain lakes and cool shade under the plane tree.

  • Ski & snowboard at Vasilitsa
  • Hiking and off-road in the Pindus
  • Stay in our rooms in Aetia
